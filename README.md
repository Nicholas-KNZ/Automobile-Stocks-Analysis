# Stock Analysis of Germany’s Automobile Industry

The project examines the stock performance of major German automobile manufacturers—BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Porsche, and Volkswagen —over the last quarter. The analysis aims to address the following key questions:

Which German automobile brand achieved the most successful quarter in terms of stock performance?
What political events influenced the stock prices of these companies?
How do German automobile manufacturers compare to both international competitors and other German industries?

My analysis yields the following conclusions:

While all German automobile companies faced a challenging quarter, Porsche's performance stands out as particularly poor. The media attributes this to ongoing issues with demand in the Chinese market. Additionally, all companies have been affected by the US tariff announcements, with potentially more adverse effects on the horizon.

Mercedes, BMW, and Volkswagen have exhibited relatively similar trends overall, although Volkswagen's stock appears slightly more stable than the other two.

On the international front, the US competitor Tesla has amplified market volatility significantly, resulting in consistent underperformance relative to German companies. In contrast, the Japanese competitor Toyota has demonstrated remarkable stability, outperforming its peers in the last quarter by showing greater resilience to the ongoing crisis.
